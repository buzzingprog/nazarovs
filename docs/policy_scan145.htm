Part 1

Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection and Use

Document and OCR Data (Local Processing & Optional Cloud)

Scan145 requires access to your device's camera or image gallery to function as a document scanner and OCR tool.

  • All core document data—including captured images, generated PDFs, customized templates, extracted text, and tracked values—is processed and stored locally on your device in an embedded database.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your scanned documents, personal records, or template configurations to our servers.
  • If you explicitly choose to use optional Cloud OCR features (such as Google Cloud Vision or Vertex AI) for enhanced accuracy, the specific images you select will be sent to those third-party services for processing, subject to their respective privacy policies.

Device and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical data necessary for functionality, updates, advertising, and related app services, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • IP address
  • App usage data via third-party services

This technical data does not include your scanned documents, extracted values, or locally stored JSON backups.

2. Permissions

The Application may request the following permissions:

  • CAMERA — to capture photos of printed documents, tables, and forms for digitization.
  • STORAGE / MEDIA — to import images from your gallery and to export multi-page PDFs or JSON backup files to your device.
  • INTERNET — for optional Cloud OCR processing, app updates, ad display, and communication with third-party services.

3. Third-Party Services

Scan145 may use third-party services for Cloud OCR, analytics, crash reporting, updates, or advertising, including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase
  • Google Cloud Vision / Vertex AI (if utilized by the user)

These third-party services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not control how these third parties handle your data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your scanned PDFs, extracted OCR data, trend history, and custom templates remain securely on your device.
  • You can modify, export, or delete this data at any time within the app or through your device's file manager.
  • Uninstalling the app may remove all app-related local data, including your embedded database and tracking history. We highly recommend using the JSON backup feature before uninstalling if you wish to keep your data.

5. Data Security

Data used by the core scanning and tracking functionality is stored locally on your device. Its security depends in part on your device's protection, such as a screen lock, system security, and device encryption.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the Application, on the app listing page, or in an updated version of this document.
