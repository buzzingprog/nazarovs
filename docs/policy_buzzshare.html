Effective date: August 31, 2026

Application covered: BuzzShare (com.rnazarov.buzzshare) for Android

Contact: support@hcontacts.com

The short version. BuzzShare is a service that runs on our own servers, so unlike a purely offline app it does hold data for you: your phone number, the posts you share, the people you have connected with, and a record of who passed which post to whom. That record is what the app is for. We show no advertising, include no analytics or tracking SDKs, and do not sell or rent your data to anyone. Your address book is not uploaded — contact names and email addresses never leave your phone; only phone numbers are checked against registered users and then discarded. Anything you post can be forwarded onward by the people who receive it, so treat a post as public.

Please read this document before installing. By installing or using BuzzShare, you agree to it. "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer of BuzzShare; "the app" means the BuzzShare Android application and the servers it connects to.

Part I — Privacy Policy

1. What We Collect and Why

BuzzShare is an online service. To work at all, it stores the following on our servers. There is nothing here that we collect "just in case" — each item is used to run a feature you can see.

DataWhy it exists
Your phone number (in international +E.164 form) It is your account. It is how contacts find you and how posts are addressed to you. Required — the app cannot be used without it.
Nickname Optional display name you choose. Shown to people who receive your posts. You can skip it or change it at any time.
Notification token An anonymous device token issued by Google, used only to deliver a push notification when something arrives for you. It identifies a device installation, not a person.
Last-activity timestamp A single "last seen" time, so the app knows whether to push a notification or you are already looking at the screen. No history of your sessions is kept.
Your connections Which accounts you have connected with, and whether the connection is confirmed by both sides. Posts can only be sent between two people who both agreed.
Posts you create The text (up to 500 characters) and the optional image you attach. This is the content you chose to share.
Distribution records For each time a post is passed on: who sent it, who received it, when, which earlier delivery it came from, and whether you have read or hidden it. This is the chain the app shows you, and the count behind "what's hot".

What we do not collect. No password. No email address. No real name, birthday, or gender. No location — the app requests no location permission and derives none. No camera or microphone access. No payment details; the app is free and contains no purchases. No advertising identifier. No behavioural profile, and no data collected for advertising purposes by us or anyone else.

2. Your Device Contacts

With your permission, the app reads your device address book so it can tell you which of the people you already know are on BuzzShare. This is worth being precise about:

  • Contact names, email addresses, photos and notes never leave your device. They are read into the app's memory to draw the contact list on screen, and that is all.
  • Only the phone numbers, converted to international format, are sent to our server to be compared against registered accounts. The server answers with which of them have an account and what your connection status is, and the submitted list is discarded — it is not written to our database, not kept as a record of your social graph, and not used to build a profile of people who are not users.
  • We never message, invite, or otherwise contact the people in your address book. Non-users are told nothing.
  • The permission is optional. Deny it and the address-book feature stops working; the rest of the app continues to run.

3. What You Post, and Who Can See It

Treat every post as public. BuzzShare exists to let posts travel. When you send a post to someone, they can send it onward to their own contacts, who can send it onward again. A post can therefore reach people you do not know and have never connected with. Do not post anything private, sensitive, or anything you would not be comfortable seeing spread beyond your circle.

  • Images you attach are uploaded to our server and served from a web address with a long random filename. That address is not listed or searchable, but anyone who obtains the link can open the image without using the app. Remove anything you do not want seen before you attach it.
  • Hiding is not deleting. Hiding a post removes it from your own screen. It does not remove copies already delivered to other people, and it does not erase the record that the post was passed along — that record is the trust chain the app is built on and is kept unchanged by design.
  • Your nickname and phone number are visible to people you are connected with, and your nickname appears to those further down a chain as part of a post's history.

4. Notifications

Push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which is used as a delivery pipe and nothing else. The app uses no other Firebase product — no Firebase sign-in, no Firebase database, no Firebase file storage, and no Firebase analytics. Google receives the device token and the short notification text in order to deliver it, and handles that under Google's own privacy policy. Denying the notification permission simply means you see new posts when you open the app.

  • Our hosting provider — the servers holding the data described in section 1 are rented from a commercial hosting company. They provide the machine; they are not given access to your data for any purpose of their own.
  • Google — for notification delivery (section 4) and, separately, as the operator of Google Play, which distributes the app and may collect installation and crash information under its own policy. That collection is Google's, not ours.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data, and we share it with no advertiser, data broker, or analytics company. We would disclose data only where legally compelled to, or where strictly necessary to protect someone's safety.

6. Permissions

Each permission is listed below with its purpose. Runtime permissions can be revoked at any time in Android Settings; the feature that depends on one will stop working and the rest of the app will continue.

PermissionTypeWhy it is needed

The app requests no access to your location, camera, microphone, SMS, call log, or telephony features. A small number of additional entries are added to the app package automatically by the development framework it is built with and back no user-facing feature.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your account and its data are kept for as long as the account exists.
  • You can remove a connection at any time, which stops posts flowing between you and that person, and you can hide any post from your own inbox.
  • To delete your account, write to support@hcontacts.com from, or quoting, the phone number on the account. We will delete the account, your phone number, your nickname, your notification token and the posts you authored, together with the delivery records attached to those posts, within 30 days.
  • Two honest limits. Copies of a post that were already delivered to other people's devices are outside our reach. And where you passed on someone else's post, the record that the post moved along that step may be retained, without your identifying details, so that the other participants' chains do not break.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it and its local data from your device but does not by itself delete your account on the server — use the request above for that.

8. Data Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS). Sessions use a signed token stored in the app's private storage on your device; the database and cache servers are not reachable from the public internet. On your device, the app's data sits inside Android's per-app sandbox. No security measure is perfect: we cannot guarantee against every breach, and the security of your device — its lock screen, its updates, and whatever else you install on it — remains yours. If a breach ever affects your data, we will say so on this page and on the app's Google Play listing.

9. Children's Privacy

BuzzShare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to support@hcontacts.com and we will remove it.
