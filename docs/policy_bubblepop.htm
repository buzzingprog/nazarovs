Part 1

Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection and Use

Game Progress and Settings (Local Processing Only)

Innastik's Bubble Pop is designed to be fully playable offline.

  • All core game data—including your level progress, star ratings, personal bests, visual theme preferences, and local multiplayer (hot-seat) results—is processed and stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your gameplay progress or customized settings to our servers.

Device and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical data necessary for functionality, updates, advertising, and related app services, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • IP address
  • App usage data via third-party services

2. Permissions

The Application may request the following permissions:

  • STORAGE / MEDIA — to save your level progress, settings, and game state locally on your device.
  • INTERNET — for app updates, ad display, and communication with third-party analytics or crash reporting services.

3. Third-Party Services

Innastik's Bubble Pop may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, updates, or advertising, including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase

These third-party services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not control how these third parties handle your data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your gameplay progress, unlocked levels, and preferences remain securely on your device.
  • You can clear this data at any time through your device's app management settings.
  • Uninstalling the app will remove all app-related local data, meaning your level progress and star ratings will be permanently lost unless backed up by your device's system.

5. Data Security

Data used by the game is stored locally on your device. Its security depends in part on your device's protection, such as a screen lock, system security, and device encryption.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the Application, on the app listing page, or in an updated version of this document.
