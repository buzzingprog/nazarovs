Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection and Use

Scanned Documents, Images, PDF Files, and OCR Data

Hemo145 allows users to scan, import, process, analyze, and store laboratory results and related medical or health-related documents within the Application.

  • Users may scan paper documents using the device camera.
  • Users may import images or PDF files for text extraction and analysis.
  • The Application may extract text, numbers, markers, values, ranges, and similar structured information from those files.
  • The extracted content may be stored locally on the device and may also be stored on our backend servers when online features, synchronization, backup, history, or cloud analysis features are used.

Local OCR and Local Processing

Some recognition and preprocessing functions may be performed locally on the device. This may include local OCR, local parsing, local temporary file processing, and related on-device operations.

  • Local processing may use technologies such as on-device OCR engines, including Tesseract or similar components.
  • When a function is processed locally, the relevant data may remain on the device unless the user uses a cloud-based feature or server-backed functionality.

Cloud OCR, AI Analysis, and Remote Processing

Some features of Hemo145 may use remote or cloud-based services for OCR, document understanding, image analysis, text extraction, structured data extraction, summarization, or related AI-assisted processing.

  • This may include Google ML Kit cloud-based features, Google Vision API, Vertex AI, and similar cloud services used by the Application.
  • When cloud features are used, images, PDF content, extracted text, document fragments, or related metadata may be transmitted to our servers and/or authorized service providers only as needed to provide the requested functionality.
  • We do not use user-scanned medical document content for selling personal data.

Stored User Data

Depending on the features used, Hemo145 may store:

  • Scanned document images
  • Imported image files
  • Imported PDF files
  • Recognized text
  • Extracted laboratory values and related structured data

Anonymous Identifiers and Technical Identifiers

Hemo145 currently does not require a traditional user account with real name, email-based registration, or public profile.

Because the Application is designed to process laboratory results and related documents, some content handled by the Application may be health-related or medical in nature. You choose what documents to scan, import, store, or analyze.
