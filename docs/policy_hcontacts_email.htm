Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Application covered: HContacts Email (com.hcontacts.email) for Android

Contact: support@hcontacts.com  |  hcontacts.com

The short version. HContacts Email keeps your mail and your contacts on your device. We operate no servers, and your messages, credentials, contacts and photos never reach us. When the app sends or receives mail it connects directly from your phone to the mail provider you chose, exactly as any standard email client does. The app does display advertising, supplied by third-party ad networks; those networks collect advertising and device data of their own, described in section 3, and they never receive your mail, your credentials or your contacts.

This policy covers HContacts Email only. The separate HContacts application (com.hcontacts.app) has its own policy; if you use both, see section 4 for what passes between them. Please read this document before installing; by installing or using the app, you agree to it.

Part I — Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our servers. We operate no servers for this application. There is no account to create with us and no sign-in to our systems. This is separate from the advertising described in section 3, which is handled by third parties and never includes the content of your mail or contacts.

Email accounts and messages. To read and send mail, the app connects over IMAP and SMTP directly from your device to the mail servers you configure — Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, or any other provider you enter. Your mail passes between your phone and your provider only; it does not pass through us.

  • Your email password or app password is stored in the device's encrypted system keystore (Android Keystore, via secure storage). It is never written to the app's database, never logged, and never sent anywhere except to your own mail provider in order to sign in.
  • Message headers (sender, subject, date) are cached on your device so the app works offline. Message bodies are downloaded only when you open a message, and are then cached on the device too.
  • The app reads your inbox. It does not create contacts from the people who email you — senders are matched against contacts you already keep, and everyone else remains ordinary mail unless you choose to add them.

Contacts. Contacts you create or edit are stored in a database on your device and are processed locally. The app does not read your device's system address book. We do not upload contacts anywhere.

Photos. If you set a contact photo, the image you select is resized and stored on your device. We do not upload or store photo data on any server.

Backups and exports. The app can export your data to a file you choose and import it back. Where that file goes — local storage, a cloud drive, a message to yourself — is your decision, and once it leaves the app it is governed by whatever service you send it to, not by this policy.

Technical and diagnostic data. We include no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software of our own, and receive no usage statistics or device identifiers from the app. Third-party advertising components collect data as described in section 3. Google Play may independently collect installation and crash information under Google's own privacy policy; that collection is Google's, not ours, and is outside our control.

2. Permissions

The app requests very little. Each permission is listed below with its single purpose. You can revoke a runtime permission at any time in Android Settings; the feature depending on it will stop working, but the rest of the app will continue to run.

PermissionTypeWhy it is needed
INTERNET Automatic To reach your mail provider over IMAP/SMTP, and to load advertisements. These are the only network connections the app makes.
Photos / media Requested when used To let you pick or take a picture for a contact's avatar. Asked only at the moment you choose an image.
com.hcontacts.permission.ACCESS_CONTACTS_DATA Granted at install A private permission shared with the HContacts app, allowing this app to read contacts from HContacts on the same device, and to add a contact there at your request. On-device only, and only meaningful if HContacts is installed.

The app requests no access to your device address book, call log, SMS, location, microphone, or telephony features.

3. Advertising

HContacts Email may display advertisements — for example a banner or an occasional full-screen ad — supplied by third-party advertising networks such as Google AdMob. Ads help keep the application free.

An advertising network operates independently of us and collects its own data in order to select, deliver, and measure ads. That typically includes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), which you can reset or delete in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads;
  • device and network information — model, operating system version, language, screen size, mobile network or connection type, and IP address (from which coarse, city-level location may be inferred);
  • ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped.

What advertising never receives. Ad networks have no access to your mailbox, the messages cached on your device, your email addresses or passwords, your contacts, or your photos. The app does not send any of that to an ad network, and it does not target ads using the content of your mail. No advertising component is given the ability to read the application's database or the device keystore.

Your controls. You can limit ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings; you can review Google's advertising practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. If a paid or ad-free version of the app is offered, choosing it removes the advertising components. Data collected by an ad network is governed by that network's own privacy policy, not by this one.

4. Third-Party Services and the HContacts App

The parties involved besides you and us are:

  • Your email provider — whichever service you configure. Your mail and your credentials are handled by them under their own privacy policy and terms. We have no visibility into that relationship and no access to your mailbox.
  • Advertising networks — as described in section 3, under their own policies.
  • Google Play — distributes the application and may collect installation and diagnostic data under its own policy.
  • Any service you export to — if you send a backup file somewhere, that destination's terms apply to the copy you sent.

The HContacts app. If you also install HContacts (com.hcontacts.app), this app can read the contacts held there so that mail from those people is recognised, and can add one contact to HContacts when you explicitly ask it to. This exchange happens entirely on your device, between two apps you installed, through Android's standard inter-app data mechanism protected by the permission listed in section 2. Nothing leaves the device, and neither app requires the other to function.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control its lifetime completely:

  • Edit or delete any contact, group, or cached message at any time inside the app.
  • Removing an email account deletes that account's cached messages and its stored password from the device keystore.
  • Uninstalling the application removes the data it stored. Mail on your provider's servers is not affected — deleting a cached message in the app does not delete it from your mailbox.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for you to request that we delete. To have advertising data removed, contact the ad network under its own policy, or reset your advertising identifier as described in section 3.

6. Data Security

All application data is stored locally on your device and is protected by Android's per-app sandbox. Email passwords are held in the device's encrypted system keystore rather than in the application database. Connections to mail servers use TLS (SSL or STARTTLS) as configured for your provider. No security measure is perfect, and the security of the device itself — its lock screen, its operating system updates, and any software you install on it — remains your responsibility.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. No data reaches us from the app, so there is none to identify or remove. Because the app carries advertising, it is not offered as a child-directed application.
