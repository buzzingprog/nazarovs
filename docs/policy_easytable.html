Part I — Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. We operate no servers for this application. There is no sign-up, no login, no user profile, and no synchronisation service. We have no way of knowing who uses the app, what tables you keep in it, or what you put in them.

Your tables. Folders, tables, columns, rows, cell values, summary rows, links between tables, and per-table display settings are stored in a database file inside the application's own private storage on your device (SQLite, via expo-sqlite). They are read and written locally. They are not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with any third party.

Files you import. When you import a CSV or XLSX file, you choose that file through Android's own document picker. The app reads the file you selected in order to build a table from it, and reads nothing else. The picker grants access to that one file only; the app does not browse or index your storage.

Files you export. When you export a table as CSV, XLSX, or PDF, the file is generated on your device and handed to Android's standard share sheet. Where it then goes — a local folder, a cloud drive, a chat, an email to yourself — is your decision, and once it leaves the app it is governed by whatever service you sent it to, not by this policy. PDF rendering also happens locally on the device; no document is sent anywhere to be converted.

Technical and diagnostic data. The app includes no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising components, no advertising identifier, and no tracking software of any kind. We receive no usage statistics, no device identifiers, and no crash reports. Google Play may independently collect installation and crash information under Google's own privacy policy; that collection is Google's, not ours, and is outside our control. If your device has Android's system backup enabled, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup — that is a function of your device and your Google account, under Google's policy, not something the app sends to us.

Example data. On first launch the app creates a sample table so you can see how it works. It is ordinary local data: rename it, edit it, or delete it like any other table.

The application requests no access to your contacts, call log, SMS, location, camera, microphone, telephony features, or accounts on the device.

3. No Advertising and No Tracking

Easy Table displays no advertisements. It contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no third-party tracking component. Nothing in the app profiles you, builds an interest segment, or reads your advertising identifier. There is no advertising data for you to opt out of, because none is collected.

4. Third-Party Services

Aside from you and us, the parties that can be involved are:

  • Google Play — distributes the application and may collect installation and diagnostic data under its own policy.
  • Any app or service you export or share to — if you send an exported table somewhere, that destination's terms apply to the copy you sent.
  • Your device's own backup — if Android backup is enabled on your device, Google's terms govern that backup.

The app embeds no third-party service that receives your data, and integrates with no cloud storage, account, or synchronisation provider.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control its lifetime completely:

  • Edit or delete any row, summary row, column, table, or folder at any time inside the app.
  • Deleting a table removes its rows and cells from the device database.
  • Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the application, removes the data it stored. Files you already exported elsewhere are not affected.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for you to ask us to delete.

6. Data Security

All application data is stored locally and is protected by Android's per-app sandbox, which keeps it unreadable to other applications on the device. Because the app has no server component and transmits nothing, there is no transmission to intercept and no online store of your data to breach. No security measure is perfect, and the security of the device itself — its lock screen, its operating system updates, and any software you install on it — remains your responsibility. An exported file is an ordinary unencrypted document; treat it with the same care as any other file containing your data.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included; no data reaches us from the app, so there is none to identify or remove.
